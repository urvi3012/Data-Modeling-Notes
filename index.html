Data Engineering Modeling

From raw transactions to analytical gold. These notes cover the essential blueprint for building modern data warehouses.

1. OLTP vs OLAP

OLTP

Online Transactional Processing. Designed for operational speed. Think of an ATM machine or an E-commerce checkout process. It records daily business operations in real-time.

OLAP

Online Analytical Processing. Designed for business intelligence. Think of a CEO asking "What was our revenue growth in Q3 compared to Q2?". It aggregates historical data for insights.

Criteria OLTP (Transactional) OLAP (Analytical)
Purpose Fast CRUD (Create, Read, Update, Delete) Complex queries and reporting
Data Format Highly Normalized (many small tables) Denormalized (Star/Snowflake)
Architecture Source System (Entity Relationship) Data Warehouse (Fact/Dimensions)
Performance Optimized for quick writes Optimized for mass data scans (reads)
Requirements Data integrity & consistency Query performance & easy visualization
Products PostgreSQL, MySQL, Oracle, SQL Server Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Databricks
